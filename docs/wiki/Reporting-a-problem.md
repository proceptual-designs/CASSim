# Reporting a problem

Tell your teacher, or whoever gave you the CASSim link.

Helpful details:

- Which app: Nspire, Pseudocode & Python or Scientific (and, for Scientific, which calculator's keys)
- What you typed or pressed (copy the entry or the program) and what CASSim showed
- What the real calculator gives, if you know
- The settings: the Nspire's Document Settings (angle, calculation mode, real or complex, digits), or the Scientific calculator's SETUP
- Which site you were using (see [[Home]]), and your device and browser
- The version, from **CASSim ▸ About**
