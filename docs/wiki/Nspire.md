# Nspire calculator

CASSim's Nspire app simulates the TI-Nspire CX CAS for VCE Mathematical Methods and Specialist Mathematics. It is not affiliated with Texas Instruments and is not an approved exam calculator.

Open it from the Home screen, or go straight to it: <https://cassim-app.pages.dev/#/app/nspire>
