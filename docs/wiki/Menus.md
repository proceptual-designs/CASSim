# Menus

Menus are numbered as on the handheld: press a number to choose that item, or use the arrow keys and Enter, or click. On a touch screen, drag a long menu up or down with one finger to scroll it (the mouse wheel does the same).

## Menu (F2)

Actions, Number, Algebra, Calculus, Probability, Statistics, Matrix & vector, Finance, Functions & programs. Pages that are not built yet have no Menu.

Some items open a small form instead of expecting you to type the command, for example the distributions, regressions, Create Matrix, systems of equations, polynomial roots and the Finance Solver. Greyed-out items are on the handheld but not built yet in CASSim.

## Doc (F4)

- **File:** new document, open and save documents kept in this browser, import and export `.cassim.json` files, copy a share link
- **Edit:** undo, redo, copy and paste
- **View:** Page sorter and other views
- **Insert:** add a Problem or a page (Calculator and Lists & Spreadsheet work so far)
- **Page layout:** delete a page
- **Settings & status:** Document settings and Handheld setup
- **8 Login** and **9 Press-to-Test** are unavailable placeholders.

## CASSim

The **CASSim** button is in the Computer toolbar and above **Doc** on the Handheld keypad.
It is the same menu, in the same order, in every CASSim app and on Home:

- **Home**, in the all-in-one app
- the views (● marks the one you are in) and the panels you can show or hide (✓ when shown):
  here **Computer view**, **Handheld view** and **Keypad**; then **Fullscreen** (on an installed
  phone, **Avoid cutout**, ✓ while the calculator keeps clear of the camera cutout)
- **Settings…** and **Appearance…**
- **Install CASSim…** or **Uninstall CASSim…**, **Check for updates** and the offline status
- **Help (wiki)…** and **About CASSim…**

Items you cannot use at the moment are greyed out rather than removed, so the numbers stay the
same. On the Handheld keypad, ctrl then CASSim offers direct installation when the browser
supports it.

A red dot on the button means an update is ready or offline setup failed; hover over it to see
which. Choose the offline status for details, and **Retry** there if setup failed. **Reload for
Update** asks first if your work has unsaved changes; updates are never applied automatically.

## Other menus

Templates opens the template grid; ctrl then Templates inserts `:=`. Ctrl then Catalog opens the
symbols, and ctrl then = opens the relations (<, ≤, ≠ and so on). The Trig, Catalog and
Punctuation keys open their own menus. In the π, Trig or Templates grid, press the same key again
to highlight the next item (wrapping at the end), then press Enter to choose it.
