# Menus

Menus are numbered as on the handheld: press a number to choose that item, or use the arrow keys and Enter, or click. On a touch screen, drag a long menu up or down with one finger to scroll it (the mouse wheel does the same).

## Menu (F2)

Actions, Number, Algebra, Calculus, Probability, Statistics, Matrix & Vector, Finance, Functions & Programs, then CASSim's own **A About CASSim** and **B Help**. Every page's Menu has About and Help under A and B, including the pages that are not built yet.

Some items open a small form instead of expecting you to type the command, for example the distributions, regressions, Create Matrix, systems of equations, polynomial roots and the Finance Solver. Greyed-out items are on the handheld but not built yet in CASSim.

## Doc (F4)

- **File:** new document, open and save files, copy a share link
- **Edit:** undo, redo, copy and paste
- **View:** Page Sorter and other views
- **Insert:** add a Problem or a page (only Calculator pages work so far)
- **Page Layout:** delete a page
- **Settings & Status:** Document Settings, Handheld Setup and About CASSim

## Other menus

Ctrl then Templates gives the template grid, ctrl then Catalog the symbols, and ctrl then = the relations (<, ≤, ≠ and so on). The Trig, Catalog and Punctuation keys open their own menus.
